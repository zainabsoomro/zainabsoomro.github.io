About

I am a data scientist focused on applied machine learning in healthcare and public-facing analytics. At Indus Hospital and Health Network, I established the data science department and work with clinical and operational teams to structure emergency-care data, monitor trends, and ship models into real workflows.

Before that, I completed an M.S. in Applied Data Science at the University of Southern California as a Fulbright scholar and led multimodal research on early ALS detection at iLab USC. Earlier in my career, I implemented analytical chatbot systems at Reckitt and published large-scale social media studies on COVID-19 and Brexit.
